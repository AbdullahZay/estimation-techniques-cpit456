COCOMO

Effort grows faster than size, and how much faster depends on the kind of project. Basic COCOMO needs only the size; the intermediate model multiplies that first estimate by cost driver ratings.

Intermediate

Rate only the drivers that really affect your project. Leave the rest at 1.00 — that is the average rating, and it changes nothing. Typical values run from 0.9 to 1.4.

Delphi

Experts estimate every task on their own, with no discussion. The coordinator compares the highest and lowest figure for each task: anything that spreads further than the agreed limit goes back for another round.

Variance is the gap between the two estimates measured against the larger one: (maximum − minimum) ÷ maximum. A task exactly on the limit is accepted; only estimates above it are rejected.
